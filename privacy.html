Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 1, 2026  ·  Last revised: May 1, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Chiki ("we," "our," or "the application") handles information when you use our mobile application. We built Chiki on a straightforward principle: your financial data belongs to you and no one else. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal financial information to any third party.

Please read this policy carefully. By using Chiki, you agree to the practices described herein.

Data Storage

All Data Stays on Your Device

Chiki stores all financial data — including accounts, transactions, budgets, savings goals, and loans — exclusively in a local database on your device. No data is transmitted to any remote server operated by us or any third party. We do not maintain back-end servers that receive or process your financial records.

What We Store Locally

  • Bank account names, types, and balances you enter manually
  • Transaction records including amounts, categories, dates, and notes
  • Monthly budget limits and category allocations
  • Savings goals, target amounts, and progress
  • Loan details including principal, balance, interest rate, and payment schedule
  • App preferences such as selected theme and notification settings

Your financial figures are never transmitted over the internet by Chiki. The data pipeline begins and ends on your device.

Chiki Chat Assistant

Chiki includes a built-in chat assistant that accepts natural language commands — such as recording a transfer, adding income, or logging an expense. All command processing is performed entirely on your device using rule-based logic. No text you type into the chat is transmitted to any server, and no external AI or language model service is used. Any financial changes made through the chat assistant are stored locally in the same on-device database as all other app data.

Biometric Authentication

How It Works

Chiki offers an optional biometric lock (Face ID, Touch ID, or device fingerprint) to restrict access to the application. This feature is implemented entirely through the device operating system's secure authentication APIs. We do not have access to any biometric data, nor do we store, process, or transmit it in any form.

Biometric enrollment and verification occur within the device's secure enclave. Chiki only receives a pass/fail result from the operating system and never sees the underlying biometric data.

Third-Party Services

In-App Purchases

Chiki offers an optional one-time premium purchase managed through RevenueCat. When you initiate a purchase, transaction data is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store) in accordance with their respective privacy policies. RevenueCat may receive anonymized purchase receipt data to verify subscription status. RevenueCat does not receive your financial records stored within the app.

We encourage you to review:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Push Notifications

If you enable notifications (bill reminders, budget alerts, or weekly summaries), the app uses the device notification system provided by Apple (APNs) or Google (FCM). These services may associate a device token with your installation. No financial data is included in notification payloads.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

Chiki does not currently integrate any third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs. No behavioral data or usage telemetry is collected.

Data Sharing

Export and Backup

Chiki allows you to export your transaction history to a CSV file and create full data backups via Settings → Data. These files are generated locally on your device and shared only through your device's native share sheet — you control where they go. We do not receive, intercept, or store any exported or backed-up data.

We will never sell, rent, license, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing or any other purpose.

Device Permissions

The application may request the following device permissions:

  • Biometrics / Face ID — used to authenticate you before granting access to the app.
  • Notifications — used to deliver bill reminders, budget alerts, and weekly summaries, if enabled by you.

All permissions are requested only when needed and can be revoked at any time through your device settings.

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect the data stored on your device. Sensitive settings such as biometric preferences and encryption keys are stored in the device's secure keychain (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), which are operating-system-level secure storage mechanisms not accessible to other applications.

Because all data resides locally on your device, the security of your information is also dependent on your device's own security measures — such as screen lock, operating system updates, and physical access controls. We recommend keeping your device software up to date and enabling a device passcode.

Children's Privacy

Chiki is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take prompt steps to address the situation.

Your Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us, you have complete control over it at all times. You may:

  • View, edit, or delete any record directly within the app
  • Export your transaction history to CSV via Settings → Data → Export to CSV
  • Create and restore full data backups via Settings → Data → Backup
  • Delete all data permanently via Settings → Danger Zone → Delete All Data
  • Uninstall the application, which removes all locally stored data from your device

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last revised" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will provide notice within the application. Continued use of Chiki after any revision constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
